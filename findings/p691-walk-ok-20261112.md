The ~487k OOM wall is not the walk's true limiter. Both walks completed at probe 404,992, UNDER the wall. What varies per load is the PH2/PH3 likelihood-order seed: most draws rank SELF beyond the probe budget and die at the wall; the rare draws whose ordering front-loads the SELF region complete the walk before the ceiling. Repro recipe therefore: **reload-battery draws (triangle reloads on a warm heap) until the seed lottery ranks SELF in-budget** — observed twice per ~70 draws degraded, no cold boot required (cold boot just skews the first draw).
